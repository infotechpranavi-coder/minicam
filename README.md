# Sentinel Gadgets

I want to build a professional, modern, multi-page e-commerce-style website for a company that sells mini covert security cameras and surveillance gadgets — things like pocket cameras, pen cameras, button cameras, keychain cameras, socket/charger cameras, night-vision watch cameras, WiFi hidden cameras, USB/power-bank cameras, GPS trackers, and camera detectors.

Tech: React + Tailwind CSS, fully responsive, e-commerce catalog structure.

Site structure (multiple pages with proper navigation):

Home: strong hero section (dark, tech/security aesthetic) with a clear headline and "Shop Now" CTA, trust badges (free shipping, COD, secure payment, warranty), a featured products carousel, a category grid (Pocket Cameras, Pen Cameras, Button Cameras, WiFi Cameras, Night Vision, Trackers, Detectors), a "why choose us" features section, best-sellers, customer testimonials, and a newsletter/offer banner.

Shop (All Products): a filterable and sortable product grid — filter by category, price, and features (night vision, WiFi, battery life) — plus search and pagination.

Product Detail Page: image gallery, full specs table (resolution, storage capacity, battery life, connectivity, size), pricing, quantity selector, add-to-cart/buy-now buttons, related products, and customer reviews.

Category Pages: dedicated landing page per product category with a banner and filtered product grid.

About Us: brand story, our mission around personal/property security and peace of mind, our quality-testing process.

How It Works: simple visual steps explaining setup, app pairing, and SD card/storage usage.

Testimonials: dedicated reviews page with ratings and customer quotes.

Contact Us: contact form, phone/WhatsApp, email, address, embedded map, FAQ accordion.

FAQ: shipping, returns, warranty, and usage-guidance questions.

Look & feel:

Color palette: deep charcoal/near-black background with an electric/steel blue accent and white text — modern tech and security feel, professional and trustworthy rather than gimmicky "spy movie" styling.

Typography: a bold modern sans-serif for headings and a clean, highly readable sans-serif for body text.

Product cards with hover zoom effects, badges like "Best Seller" / "New" / "HD 1080p", a sticky navbar with search and cart icon, and subtle scroll-in animations.

Use clean, professional product-style and tech-close-up imagery — think mainstream consumer electronics store quality, not sensational or clip-art "spy" visuals.

Persistent floating WhatsApp/Call button, and a top promo bar (e.g. "Free shipping over ₹999").

Footer with organized columns (Shop, Company, Support, Policies), payment method icons, social icons, and a newsletter signup.

The overall vibe should be a legitimate, well-designed consumer electronics/security-gadgets e-commerce brand — polished, credible, tech-forward — comparable in design quality to a mainstream gadget or electronics store.
